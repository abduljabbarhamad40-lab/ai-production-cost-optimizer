# AI Production Cost Optimizer

Choose the lowest-cost reliable workflow that meets the quality requirement. Prefer genuinely free tiers when suitable, but distinguish recurring free allocations from trials and promotional credits.

## For every candidate verify
- Official free allocation, reset period, and model-specific rate limits
- Model capability and output quality
- Region/account eligibility and billing requirements
- Commercial-use terms and data handling
- Watermarks, resolution, duration, formats, and export restrictions
- Overage pricing and fallback options

## Workflow
1. Define the deliverable and quality bar.
2. Eliminate candidates failing hard requirements such as rights, format, region, watermark, or quality.
3. Compare total cost per usable output, not headline pricing.
4. Prefer deterministic local editing and reusable assets when a model is unnecessary.
5. Batch similar requests and cache reusable outputs where permitted.
6. Keep a fallback for outages and quota exhaustion.
7. State uncertainty, official sources, and verification date.

## Priority providers
Start with Gemini API, Cloudflare Workers AI, and Hugging Face Inference Providers. Expand when another reputable service offers a clear advantage. Include API, no-code, and local tools where useful.

## Cost and security
No automatic paid upgrades, purchases, billing activation, or API-key changes. Never commit secrets. Do not assume all models share one quota. Show estimated cost ranges and assumptions. If no suitable free tool exists, use the creative fallback skill.

## Output
Provide a ranked shortlist with task fit, quality, free limits, restrictions, commercial rights, region/billing, overage cost, verification date, official source, and recommended workflow.

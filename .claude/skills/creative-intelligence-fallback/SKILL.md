# Creative Intelligence Fallback

Do not stop at saying a tool is unavailable. Convert the limitation into a practical creative solution that preserves the quality target.

## Workflow
1. Define deliverable, audience, platform, aspect ratio, duration, language, tone, and quality bar.
2. Identify the blocker: capability, quota, region, watermark, rights, latency, quality, or budget.
3. Separate essential requirements from optional effects.
4. Build a production path: original script, scene breakdown, image prompts, voiceover, sound cues, edit instructions, captions, thumbnail, and publishing copy.
5. Reuse assets and batch similar requests to reduce calls.
6. Deliver a production-ready package.

## Deliverables
Concept and hook; synopsis; timed script; scene-by-scene shot list; character/location continuity sheet; prompts per shot; voiceover pacing notes; sound design; editing timeline; captions; thumbnail and title options; description, hashtags, CTA; production checklist and estimated tool/call budget.

Never silently downgrade quality or fabricate renders, licensing, tool outputs, or availability. Do not copy protected work or promise revenue.
